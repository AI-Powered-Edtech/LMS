### A3 — partial enforce flip (HIGH leverage, LOW risk)
**Goal:** Stop logging "would_deny" and start actually 403'ing for the modules where the rbac matrix has been clean.
**Files:** `crates/middleware/src/rbac_policy.rs` (or wherever `shadow_mode` is read), `config/rbac_policy.yaml`.

1. Add a per-module enforce list to `rbac_policy.yaml` (e.g. `enforce: [finance, audit, admin_users, counseling]`) — leave other modules in shadow.

3. Re-run `rbac.spec.ts`; gate stays at `unexpected_deny = 0`.
**Estimate:** 4–6 hours.
**DoD:** A finance/BK/audit/admin_users persona crossing role boundaries gets 403 in real traffic; teacher/wali_kelas modules stay shadow.

### G2 BE — chunked SSE for AI tutor (MEDIUM leverage, MEDIUM risk)
**Goal:** Stream LLM tokens to FE; right now `/ai/tutor/stream` exists but the existing handler buffers.
**Files:** `crates/services/src/ai/tutor.rs`, `crates/api-server/src/ai_handlers.rs`.
**Approach:** swap `reqwest::Response::text()` for `bytes_stream()`, forward each chunk as an SSE `data:` line. The FE component already consumes async generators (handoff confirms G2 FE is done).
**Estimate:** 1 day. Includes a small load test to confirm backpressure works.
**DoD:** Curl-streaming the endpoint yields incremental output; `AITutorPanel` shows tokens appearing live.

### B2 full — classroomService/gradebookApi/attendanceService rombel preference (MEDIUM leverage, HIGH risk)

**Files:** `src/features/classroom/api/classSectionAdapter.ts` (already light), `src/features/classroom/services/classroomService.ts`, `src/features/gradebook/api/gradebookApi.ts`, `src/features/attendance/services/attendanceService.ts`.
